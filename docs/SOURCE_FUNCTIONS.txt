Account and Registration Management Module
This feature lets the administrators populate accounts of different users such as police, fire stations and concerned local government units and an interface where end users can register in the system.

Incident Report Management Module
This module entails the citizens to report incidents and directly transmit messages to the concerned unit. The system is focused on fire and crime incidents.
Agency Management Module
This module entails the authorized government agencies to receive the reports sent by the citizens. This module lets the personnel provide details in terms of the reports.
Report Queuing Management Module
This module entails the reports to be filtered to determine the dates of submitted reports.
Response Management Module
This module lets the submitted reports to be encoded in the system. This feature is part of the monitoring of the incident reports.

Heat Map Analysis
This involves aggregating data from various reported and validated crimes and incidents into a dashboard that management can use to provide strategic assistance. The heatmap analysis is integral to the decision support mechanism, allowing for the assessment of whether certain locations are prone to incidents.
